# SFQ-0002 Disposition After The Flint OIG Review

## Boundary

This disposition uses only `SFQ-0002`, its prior disposition, and the bounded
EPA-OIG trace review. It does not complete a missing link, infer blame or duty,
prescribe a remedy, accept a schema, change candidate-only receiver fit,
create receiver work, or request action.

## Prior Inquiry

`SFQ-0002` asks when affected-party or operational observations become
decision-relevant validation and correction objects across infrastructure and
authority boundaries. Its first disposition kept the inquiry unchanged while
requiring one independently reconstructed case trace before another
cross-case comparison.

## New Source-Review Result

`SFQ-0002-FLINT-OIG-TRACE-REVIEW-0001` returns `partial_trace_only`. It supports
dated complaint evidence, the OIG's Region 5 record source, and receipt of 87
complaints by Region 5 before the January 2016 order. It leaves custody of one
selected observation, recipient authority then in force, linkage to a current
validation burden, source-preserved disposition, a timely unresolved stop,
and causal relevance `unknown`.

## Disposition

`continue_unmodified`.

The partial trace strengthens the evidence that an observation may exist and
reach an institutional recipient without proving the decision-time chain the
inquiry asks about. That is a discriminating result, but it neither answers
the inquiry nor supports broadening it. The inquiry remains useful in its
current form; this result does not justify another intermediate Flint review
contract or a shared-mechanism claim.

## Scope Retained

- observation type and date;
- affected standing separate from custody, receipt, and authority;
- source-preserved custody, recipient, and disposition transitions;
- then-current validation burden and decision timing;
- accountable unresolved stop or correction condition; and
- drinking-water, health, civil-rights, regulatory, and legal absorbers.

## Scope Refused

This disposition refuses to treat aggregate complaint receipt, later
tracking, escalation, intervention recommendations, or missing links as proof
of one review-chain mechanism. It refuses facts borrowed from another case,
schema acceptance, remedy selection, receiver work, and external action.
Mechanism Design receiver fit remains candidate-only.

## Evidence Still Required

One source-preserving case trace must connect a named observation and date to
its custodian, accountable recipient and authority then in force, current
validation burden, source-preserved disposition, timely unresolved stop or
correction condition, and causal relevance. Every unsupported link remains
`unknown`.

## Falsifier

Narrow, defer, or retire the inquiry if independent case traces repeatedly
cannot be reconstructed without borrowing facts; if the relevant observations
were timely connected to current validation and dispositioned by accountable
owners; or if the preserved domain explanation absorbs the proposed chain
without an additional cross-boundary failure.

DISPOSITION SFQ-0002 AFTER ONE PARTIAL FLINT TRACE — NO SCHEMA, REMEDY, ACCEPTANCE, RECEIVER-WORK, OR EXTERNAL-ACTION CHANGE
