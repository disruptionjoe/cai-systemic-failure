# East Palestine Decision-Information Trace Review

## Boundary

This review uses only the corrected National Transportation Safety Board
report `RIR-24-05`, especially report pages 19-23 and 151-157. It traces one
post-derailment information-to-authority decision object. It does not merge
that object with the earlier bearing-detection phase, supply missing Flint or
Texas links, broaden `SFQ-0002`, infer individual intent, blame, duty,
liability, or health causation, prescribe a remedy, accept a schema, create
receiver work, or request action.

Derailment and Hazardous Materials Release, East Palestine, Ohio, February 3,
2023*, `RIR-24-05`, corrected July 23, 2024:

## Disposition

`bounded_decision_information_trace_supported`.

The report supports a source-preserving supplier-to-contractor-to-incident-
command gap, one named accountable decision authority, a bounded alternative
set, a final disposition, and source-stated relevance. Complete custody,
every participant's knowledge, the complete feasible alternative set, and all
legal, health, and remedy effects remain unknown.

## Relation To SFQ-0002

The trace is a distinct authority-conversion pressure relevant to
`SFQ-0002`'s questions about custody, accountable receipt, disposition, and
stop conditions. It is not an affected-party complaint or a Texas operational-
knowledge chain, does not replace either source record, and cannot fill their
missing links. A later disposition may test whether it sharpens the inquiry
without broadening its observation classes or candidate-only receiver fit.

## Domain Absorbers And Falsifier

Rail operations, hazardous-material transport, tank-car condition, chemical
stability, emergency response, incident command, public health, regulation,
and legal accounts remain sovereign. Narrow or refute this trace if closer
source review shows that Oxy Vinyls' assessment reached the full incident
command before the decision, that the incident commander did not retain final
authority, that the NTSB did not connect the communication gap to the decision,
or that the claimed relation requires borrowing from the bearing phase.
