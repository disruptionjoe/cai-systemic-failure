# Inquiries

An inquiry must be answerable, source-backed, relevant to a material failure,
explicit about uncertainty and falsifiers, and routed to the lowest sovereign
owner that fits. Unowned integrative solution questions may be proposed to CAI
Mechanism Design through the common envelope.
