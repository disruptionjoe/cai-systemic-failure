# SFQ-0002 Receiver-Readiness Disposition

## Boundary

This disposition asks only whether the current bounded Flint and Texas source
chains, sharpened by the distinct East Palestine authority-conversion
pressure, are complete enough to open receiver review of `SFQ-0002`.

It does not fill a missing source link, merge the cases, transfer standing,
establish recurrence or a shared mechanism, broaden the inquiry, accept a
schema, assign blame or duty, prescribe a remedy, create receiver work, or
request external action.

## Supported Result

The Flint review supports dated complaint evidence, its EPA Region 5 record
source, and receipt by Region 5. Decision-time custody, authority, validation,
disposition, stop, and causal links remain unknown. The Texas record preserves
prior inquiries and operating evidence, but a complete accountable knowledge-
to-validation and stop chain remains unproved.

East Palestine supplies one distinct authority-conversion pressure: source-
backed expertise can remain fragmented before reaching the final decision
authority. That pressure sharpens existing custody, accountable-receipt,
disposition, and stop-condition questions. It fills neither the Flint nor the
Texas chain.

## Disposition

`require_source_local_completion_before_receiver`.

Keep `SFQ-0002` unmodified and keep Mechanism Design fit candidate-only. A
receiver review is premature until the relevant source-local chain is complete
enough to preserve observation, custody, accountable authority, current
validation burden, disposition, and stop or correction status without borrowing
facts across cases.

## Evidence Still Required

For Flint, seek one source-local object that can add a named decision-time link
without turning the later emergency order or later recommendations into proof
of the earlier complaint chain. For Texas, require a source-local accountable
knowledge-to-validation and stop trace before reopening the deferred lineage.
Every unsupported link remains `unknown`.

## Alternatives, Counterevidence, And Falsifier

Drinking-water treatment, public health, civil rights, federal and state
oversight, electric reliability, gas supply, planning, market, emergency-
response, and legal accounts remain sovereign. Receipt, institutional
plurality, delayed response, or public harm alone is not a receiver-ready
mechanism.

Reverse this disposition only if source-local evidence independently completes
the load-bearing links for a bounded case and the proposed receiver question
cannot be fully absorbed by the case's sovereign domain account. Narrow or
retire `SFQ-0002` if repeated bounded reviews cannot reconstruct such a chain
without borrowing or if the relevant observations were already timely
validated, dispositioned, and connected to an accountable stop.
