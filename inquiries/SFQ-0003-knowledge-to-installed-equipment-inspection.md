# SFQ-0003 - Knowledge To Installed-Equipment Inspection

## Question

What decision structures allow materially relevant technical knowledge to
become installed-equipment-specific characterization, measurement, and
inspection before continued operation while preserving distinct damage
regimes, uncertainty, and accountable domain authority?

## Bounded Relation

The PES and Tesoro Anacortes records support one narrow relation. In PES,
published HF-corrosion knowledge about nickel and copper limits did not lead to
identification and inspection of every installed carbon-steel component,
including the high-nickel, high-copper elbow that later failed. In Tesoro,
API RP 941 Nelson curves and damage-mechanism reviews framed HTHA risk, while
the B and E exchangers lacked complete actual-condition measurement and were
not inspected for HTHA before the event.

The comparison supports asking how technical knowledge becomes equipment-
specific action. It does not establish one knowledge-custody, inspection-
authority, management, implementation, or control-effect mechanism.

## Case-Specific Boundaries And Standing

PES concerned HF corrosion, residual-element composition, all-component
inspection coverage, normal unit operation, five workers and one firefighter
with minor injuries, and bounded community evidence. Tesoro concerned HTHA,
Nelson-curve and operating-condition uncertainty, exchanger-specific
measurement and inspection, startup work, and seven employee fatalities.

The damage physics, guidance, measurement, inspection technique, work phase,
standing, consequence, and later recommendation objects remain separate.
Neither case may supply a missing source link for the other.

## Decision To Explain

The inquiry asks what source-visible decision structure connects a changed or
new body of technical knowledge to:

1. identification of the installed equipment to which it applies;
2. measurement of the equipment's actual material and operating condition;
3. selection and execution of an appropriate inspection;
4. accountable review and disposition before continued operation; and
5. a preserved unresolved stop or correction condition when evidence is
   incomplete.

It does not assume that one generic register, checklist, authority, or remedy
is the answer.

## Candidate Receiver Boundary

Systemic Failure owns this source-backed inquiry and its correction. Mechanism
Design fit remains candidate-only: a later governed proposal may ask that owner
to compare bounded mechanism classes, but this file creates no receiver work,

health, environmental, and legal owners retain their domain authority.

## Source-Completion Gate

Before receiver review, at least one source-local chain should connect the
relevant technical knowledge to its custodian, accountable review, installed-
equipment identification, measurement and inspection selection, disposition,
implementation state, and unresolved stop or correction condition. Complete
source-local custody, accountable review, inspection-selection authority,
disposition, implementation, relative causal weight, and control effect remain
unknown in the present comparison.

## Counterevidence And Falsifiers

Narrow or retire the inquiry if closer official-source review shows that
either case lacked the stated pre-event technical knowledge, or that the
relevant installed equipment had been completely characterized and inspected
under that knowledge before the event. Also narrow it if the two domain-
specific accounts fully absorb the apparent relation without a source-visible
decision-translation question, or if completing a chain repeatedly requires
borrowing facts across cases.

Different damage mechanisms, evidence forms, measurement problems, inspection
methods, work phases, consequences, and unresolved authority chains are
counterevidence to recurrence or one shared mechanism.

## Non-Effect

This inquiry accepts no recurrence, shared mechanism, schema, remedy,
acceptance, receiver work, blame, duty, HF or health conclusion, environmental
conclusion, liability, or external action.
