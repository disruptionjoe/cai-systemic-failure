# SFQ-0002 Affected-Observation Disposition

## Boundary

This disposition uses only `SFQ-0002` and the bounded Flint/Grenfell
comparison. It decides whether that comparison changes the inquiry; it does
not equate observation types, infer blame or legal duty, prescribe a remedy,
accept a schema, change receiver fit, create receiver work, or request action.

## Existing Inquiry

`SFQ-0002` asks when affected-party or operational observations become
decision-relevant validation and correction objects across infrastructure and
authority boundaries. Its discriminating dimensions are observation type,
affected standing, custody, recipient, authority, timing, validation burden,
disposition, and any accountable stop or correction condition.

## New Discriminating Evidence

The Flint/Grenfell comparison adds a second affected-party observation path
and makes one distinction especially visible: affected standing is not
evidence custody, review receipt, or decision authority. Flint preserves
resident complaints and independent sampling alongside utility, state,
public-health, and federal paths. Grenfell preserves resident concerns beside
separate professional, coroner, incident-learning, design, control, product,
fire-service, and government-guidance evidence.

That distinction is useful but already fits the existing inquiry. The inquiry
already requires each observation's source and standing to remain separate
from its custodian, recipient, authority, and disposition. The comparison does
not support replacing Flint/Texas, merging resident and operational evidence,
or adding a new mechanism class.

## Disposition

`continue_unmodified`.

Keep `SFQ-0002` as written. The Flint/Grenfell result is a new bounded test of
the inquiry, not a reason to broaden, split, or retire it. The next evidence
search should reconstruct one case chain independently before another cross-
case comparison: named observation, date, source and affected standing,
custodian, recipient, recipient authority, then-current validation burden,
disposition, and any timely unresolved stop.

## Scope Retained

- distinct resident-originated, technical, and operational observation types;
- affected standing separate from institutional custody and authority;
- source-preserved custody and recipient transitions;
- decision-time authority and validation burden;
- accountable disposition or unresolved stop; and
- water, health, civil-rights, building-safety, product, regulatory,
  emergency-response, electric, gas, planning, and legal absorbers.

## Scope Refused

This disposition refuses shared-harm or case-count support, observation-type
equivalence, one generic complaint mechanism, facts borrowed across cases,
schema acceptance, remedy selection, receiver work, and external action.
Mechanism Design receiver fit remains candidate-only.

## Evidence Still Required

A source-preserving case trace must name the observation and its time, source,
affected standing, custodian, recipient, recipient authority, current
validation requirement, disposition or absence of disposition, and any stop or
correction condition. Each chain must stand on its own sources before
comparison.

## Falsifier

Narrow or retire the inquiry if the chains cannot be reconstructed without
borrowing facts across cases; if the relevant observations were timely
preserved, connected to current validation, and dispositioned by accountable
owners; or if a generic observation object necessarily erases source type,
standing, authority, timing, or the stronger domain explanation.

## Candidate Effect

Continue one source-preserving inquiry without changing schema, remedy,
acceptance, receiver work, or external action.
