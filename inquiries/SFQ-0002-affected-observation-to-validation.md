# SFQ-0002 - Affected Observation To Validation Across Boundaries

## Boundary

This inquiry compares only the public-official-source traces already preserved
in `SF-0003` and `SF-0006`. It asks how affected-party or operational
observations may become decision-relevant validation and correction objects
when risk crosses infrastructure and authority boundaries.

It does not treat Flint resident evidence and Texas prior-inquiry or operating
evidence as equivalent, merge the cases, assign blame or legal duty, prescribe
a remedy, accept a schema, select an intervention, authorize receiver work, or
request external action.

## Supported Case Traces

Flint preserves a changed water source, changed corrosion-control and exposure
validation burdens, resident complaints, independent sampling, official
concern, later health evidence, emergency action, and distinct utility, state,
and federal paths. The bounded record makes affected-person standing central
without reducing the crisis to one missed message or settling engineering,
health, civil-rights, regulatory, or legal conclusions.

Texas preserves recurrent cold-weather inquiries, familiar freezing-prone
components, equipment and fuel failures, gas-electric interdependence,
generator-preparation and coordination concerns, load shedding, and regional
affected standing. The bounded record does not establish a complaint path
analogous to Flint or a complete map of who could convert prior learning into
binding validation or stop conditions.

## Material Differences

Flint includes affected-party complaints and independent sampling moving
through a drinking-water, public-health, utility, primacy, and federal-
oversight context. Texas includes prior official inquiries and operating
evidence moving through electric-reliability, generator, gas-system,
planning, regional-operator, and regulatory contexts. One case foregrounds
resident-originated evidence; the other foregrounds technical recurrence and
cross-system operating evidence. Public harm and multiple institutions are a
surface resemblance, not a shared mechanism.

## Answerable Inquiry

Under what evidence, custody, authority, and timing conditions do affected-
party or operational observations become decision-relevant validation and
correction objects when risk crosses infrastructure and authority boundaries,
and when do they remain complaints, advisory learning, or fragmented evidence
without a current accountable stop condition?

## Candidate Mechanism Classes

These are questions to discriminate, not accepted mechanisms:

- observation custody: whether a complaint, sample, incident lesson, or
  operating signal remains linked to its source, affected standing, and
  unresolved claim;
- validation-object formation: whether heterogeneous observations become one
  bounded contradiction against a named current validation burden;
- authority conversion: whether a recipient can require review, carry the
  contradiction to an accountable owner, or keep qualification stopped; and
- cross-boundary return: whether delegation across infrastructure or
  jurisdiction preserves disposition, correction, and later review.

## Evidence That Would Discriminate

For each case, seek public records that name the observation, its time and
source, the recipient, the recipient's authority, the validation requirement
then in force, the disposition or absence of disposition, and any stop or
correction condition. Compare only after each chain is independently supported.
Evidence that observations were received but were irrelevant, already
resolved, outside the recipient's authority, or absorbed by a narrower domain
account would narrow the inquiry.

## Competing Explanations And Falsifiers

Flint may remain primarily a drinking-water treatment, sampling, public-health,
civil-rights, oversight, or emergency-authority failure. Texas may remain
primarily an extreme-weather, equipment, fuel, planning, market, operating, or
resource-adequacy failure. Institutional plurality, delayed response, or
public harm alone does not establish the proposed mechanism.

Falsify or narrow the cross-case inquiry if the observation chains cannot be
reconstructed without borrowing facts across cases; if decision-relevant
observations were already preserved, dispositioned, and connected to current
validation and correction; or if one generic observation-to-validation object
erases source type, authority, affected standing, timing, or domain-specific
absorbers.
