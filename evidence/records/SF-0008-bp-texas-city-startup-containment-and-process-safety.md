## Event And Scale

During startup of a hydrocarbon isomerization unit, the raffinate splitter
tower flooded with hydrocarbons and was overpressurized. A geyser-like release
from an atmospheric blowdown and vent stack formed a flammable vapor cloud
that exploded. Fifteen workers were killed and 180 were injured. The bounded
record spans unit startup, instrumentation, containment and relief, facility
siting, workforce conditions, refinery management, corporate oversight, and
regulation.
