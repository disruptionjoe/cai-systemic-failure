# SF-0007 - East Palestine Bearing Monitoring and Emergency Information

## Boundary

This is a bounded public-source synthesis of the February 2023 Norfolk
Southern derailment and hazardous-material release in East Palestine, Ohio. It
keeps the derailment/detection phase distinct from the later emergency-
decision phase and preserves the NTSB's multi-issue account.

It does not infer individual intent, complete custody, every information
transition, legal liability, health causation, remedy efficacy, recurrence, or
schema fit. It does not assign blame or duty, prescribe a rail, hazardous-
materials, firefighting, public-health, or community remedy, count as a sixth
positive review-chain record, create receiver work, or request action.

## Event And Scale

The NTSB found that a defective wheel bearing failed and overheated, causing
the February 3 derailment and hazardous-material release. Eleven tank cars
carrying hazardous materials derailed. Three days later, the local incident
commander approved venting and burning five vinyl-chloride-monomer tank cars.
The record spans freight-rail operations, hazardous-materials transport, local
emergency response, state information needs, and an affected community.

  mitigate the bearing failure, while later information supplied to incident

  crashworthiness, placarding, and continued DOT-111 tank-car use remain

- **Scale:** freight rail, hazardous-material transport, local incident
