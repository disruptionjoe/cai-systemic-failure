## Event And Scale

Gasoline tank 912 overfilled after its remotely supervised automated level-
control gauge and an independent high-level switch capable of initiating
automatic shutdown were both inoperable. A large vapor cloud exploded, and
fire or other damage involved 22 tanks. No fatalities occurred, 43 people were
injured, nearby commercial and residential property was damaged, and the fire
burned for four days.

The record spans tank, transfer, depot, emergency response, neighboring
community, environmental resources, safety management, and COMAH regulatory
scales without treating them as one mechanism.
