## Event And Scale

A ship-to-terminal transfer of more than ten million gallons of gasoline was
distributed among four tanks over more than 24 hours. After operators directed
the remaining flow to tanks 409 and 411, tank 409 overflowed for about 26
minutes and released nearly 200,000 gallons of gasoline through its vents into
the containment dike. A roughly 107-acre vapor cloud formed and ignited,
damaging 17 of 48 tanks; multiple fires burned for almost 60 hours. The pressure
wave damaged approximately 300 nearby homes and businesses, and petroleum
entered surrounding soil, wetlands, and waterways. No fatalities occurred.

The record spans ship, dock, transfer line, receiving tanks, containment dike,
terminal operations, emergency response, neighboring community, environmental
resources, industry standards, and regulatory scales without treating them as
one mechanism.
