## Event And Scale

A carbon-steel elbow ruptured in the HF alkylation unit after accelerated HF
corrosion reduced its minimum measured wall thickness to 0.011 inch. The
release formed a vapor cloud, which ignited and led to a large fire and three
explosions. The rapid acid deinventory system routed about 339,000 pounds of HF
from the unit to a separate drum, while the remotely commanded water-mitigation
system did not start after its control communication and backup power failed.
A supervisor manually started the supplying pump later in the event.

The report estimates that 5,239 pounds of HF released from piping and
equipment, with 3,271 pounds reaching the atmosphere without water-spray
containment. A roughly 38,000-pound V-1 vessel fragment crossed the Schuylkill
River. Five workers and one firefighter experienced minor injuries, the report
records an estimated $750 million property loss, and the CSB states that it was
unaware of offsite impacts from the HF release. This record makes no exposure
or health conclusion from those bounded facts.

The record spans component, piping circuit, process unit, mitigation system,
refinery, worker, responder, neighboring community, owner, industry-standard,
trade-association, and regulator scales without treating them as one mechanism.
