## Event And Scale

ValuJet Flight 592, a Douglas DC-9-32, crashed into the Everglades about 10
minutes after takeoff from Miami. Both pilots, the 3 flight attendants, and all
105 passengers died.

The event spans component condition, maintenance removal, work-card custody,
contractor handling, company-material and hazardous-material classification,
cargo loading, fire protection, flight-crew response, carrier and federal
oversight, affected standing, and consequence without making them one

## Claim Status, Counterevidence, And Alternatives

Claim status: `investigation_synthesis`. NTSB identified the in-flight fire
initiated by one or more improperly carried chemical oxygen generators and
identified SabreTech preparation, packaging, and identification failures,
ValuJet contract-maintenance oversight, and the absence of required class D
cargo detection and suppression among the probable causes. It separately
identified FAA monitoring, prior-hazard response, and hazardous-material
awareness and training deficiencies as contributing factors. This record
preserves those findings without promoting them into recurrence or one
maintenance, hazardous-material, cargo-fire, contractor, carrier, or federal-
oversight theory.

Counterevidence to one complete mechanism includes the separation between
component condition, safety-cap status, work-card signoff, packaging and
identification, contractor-to-carrier custody, cargo loading, compartment
design, fire initiation, warning and response, carrier program oversight, and
FAA regulation and surveillance. NTSB found the electrical system was not the
source of fire ignition and treated earlier warning and suppression as added
time and spread limitation rather than proof the intense fire would have been
fully extinguished.

Alternative bounded explanations remain live: a component-handling account, a
work-card and signoff account, a hazardous-material identification and custody
account, a carrier-acceptance account, a cargo-compartment protection account,
a contract-maintenance governance account, and a regulatory-surveillance
account. The source supports relations among them for this event but not one
shared owner, authority, remedy, or recurrence mechanism.
