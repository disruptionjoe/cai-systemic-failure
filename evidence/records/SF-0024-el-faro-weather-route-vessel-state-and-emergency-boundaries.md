## Event And Scale

On October 1, 2015, during Hurricane Joaquin, the US-flagged cargo ship *El
Faro* sank about 40 nautical miles northeast of Acklins and Crooked Island,
Bahamas. All 33 people aboard died.

The event spans hurricane exposure, weather information, route selection,
