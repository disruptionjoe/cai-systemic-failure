# SF-0011 - Arkema Crosby Flood And Refrigeration Boundaries

## Boundary

This is a bounded public-source synthesis of the August and September 2017
organic-peroxide decomposition and fires at Arkema's Crosby, Texas facility.
It keeps extreme-weather flood exposure, common-mode refrigeration and backup-
power loss, organic-peroxide decomposition, and evacuation or responder
exposure distinct.

It does not reconstruct every forecast, risk-information custody path,
individual's knowledge, emergency decision, or safeguard alternative; collapse
the layers into one mechanism; determine legal duty or blame beyond the source;
establish recurrence or fit with the provisional review-chain screen; prescribe
a remedy; create receiver work; or request action.

## Event And Scale

Hurricane Harvey flooding exceeded equipment design elevations and caused loss
of facility power, backup power, and critical organic-peroxide refrigeration.
After refrigerated trailers also lost effective cooling, peroxide products
decomposed and burned. Twenty-one people sought medical attention after
exposure to decomposition fumes, and more than 200 residents within 1.5 miles
remained evacuated for a week. The bounded record spans site design,
refrigeration, emergency power, temporary cooling, process-hazard analysis,
risk information, incident command, transportation, and community exposure
without treating them as one cause.
