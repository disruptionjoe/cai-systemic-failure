# SF-0015 - Chevron Richmond Sulfidation, Inspection, And Leak Response

## Boundary

This is a bounded public-source synthesis of the August 6, 2012 crude-unit
pipe rupture and fire at the Chevron Richmond refinery. It keeps sulfidation
corrosion, component-level inspection, material selection, turnaround scope,
leak response, worker and responder exposure, community warning, industry
guidance, and regulatory oversight distinct.

It does not reconstruct every component's history, every person's knowledge,
the complete custody or implementation chain, one root cause, blame, legal
duty, health causation, recurrence, fit with the provisional review-chain
screen, remedy efficacy, receiver work, or external action.

## Event And Scale

A 52-inch component of an 8-inch crude-unit sidecut line ruptured and released
hot light gas oil, producing a vapor cloud that engulfed 19 employees before
ignition and fire. Six employees suffered minor injuries. Smoke moved offsite,
and the community received warning and shelter-in-place advice. The record
spans one piping component, a circuit, crude unit, refinery work and incident
command, corporate guidance, industry standards, regulation, workforce, and
community scales without treating them as one mechanism.
