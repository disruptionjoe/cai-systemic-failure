# SF-0020 - ExxonMobil Torrance FCC Transient-Operation Boundaries

## Boundary

This is a bounded public-source synthesis of the February 18, 2015 explosion
inside the electrostatic precipitator at the ExxonMobil Torrance refinery's
fluid catalytic cracking unit. It keeps Safe Park operating state, expander-
maintenance isolation, procedure variance, reactor steam pressure, catalyst
slide-valve condition, hydrocarbon backflow, air-side and ESP path, worker or
community standing, and industry learning distinct.

It does not reconstruct complete individual knowledge, custody, authority,
disposition, implementation, one root cause, blame, legal duty, exposure or
health causation, recurrence, fit with the provisional review-chain screen,
remedy efficacy, receiver work, or external action.

## Event And Scale

The FCC unit automatically entered ExxonMobil's idled `Safe Park` mode after
expander vibration prevented continued operation. Personnel then prepared to
isolate the expander for inspection and maintenance. During that work, reactor
steam was reduced to limit steam escaping from an open flange. Reactor
pressure fell below the level needed to prevent higher-pressure hydrocarbons
in the main column from backflowing through the reactor and an eroded spent-
catalyst slide valve into the regenerator and flue-gas system. Hydrocarbons
mixed with air and ignited inside the energized ESP.

The explosion damaged refinery equipment, caused four nearby contract workers
to seek first aid while fleeing, released catalyst dust into the nearby
community, and sent debris near modified-hydrofluoric-acid settler tanks. This
record makes no HF release or health conclusion.

The record spans valve, reactor, main column, regenerator, flue-gas system,
ESP, FCC unit, maintenance work, refinery management, workers, neighboring
community, company, industry, and regulator without treating them as one
mechanism.
