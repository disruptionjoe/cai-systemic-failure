# SF-0009 - Conception Marine-Safety Control Boundaries

## Boundary

This is a bounded public-source synthesis of the September 2, 2019 fire aboard
the small passenger vessel *Conception*. It keeps operator oversight and the
required roving patrol distinct from smoke-detection regulation and emergency
escape arrangements.

It does not determine the fire's ignition source, reconstruct a complete
review or custody chain, infer individual knowledge or intent, assign blame or
legal duty, prescribe a marine-safety remedy, establish recurrence or a shared
mechanism, count as a sixth positive review-chain record, accept a schema,
create receiver work, or request action.

Source posture: public official investigation report. The report controls this

## Event And Scale

The fire developed in the vicinity of the aft salon while passengers and one
crewmember slept below deck. Thirty-three passengers and one crewmember died.
The NTSB could not determine the exact ignition source or timing. The bounded
record spans vessel operation, overnight watchstanding, fire detection,
passenger escape, company oversight, and Coast Guard regulation without
collapsing those layers into one control.

## System Interaction Lenses

- **Information:** the absent patrol and local-only detection constrained early
  warning; complete information custody remains unknown.
- **Resources:** watchstanding, detection equipment, and escape arrangements
  are distinct operational and physical protections.
- **Authority:** operator and Coast Guard control layers are visible, but every
  decision and authority transition is not reconstructed.
- **Power:** operator and regulatory controls shaped exposure without proving a
  single power chain.
- **Participation and standing:** passengers and crew carried direct affected
  standing; their full warning and escalation paths are not reconstructed.
- **Scale:** vessel, company, inspection, and regulation interact without
  becoming one proven recurring mechanism.
- **Feedback and adaptation:** unsafe-practice identification and audit are
  source-backed pressures; closure and remedy efficacy remain unknown.
