# SF-0027 - San Bruno Pipeline Fabrication, Records, Isolation, And Oversight Boundaries

## Boundary

This is a bounded public-source synthesis of the September 9, 2010 rupture and
fire on Pacific Gas and Electric Company natural-gas transmission Line 132 in
San Bruno, California. It keeps pipe fabrication and quality control, pipeline
records, integrity management, rupture and ignition, line-break recognition
and isolation, emergency response, affected standing, regulation and
oversight, and company safety management distinct.

It does not reconstruct complete fabrication or record custody, integrity
decisions, rupture-recognition and isolation authority, response sequence,
oversight disposition, correction, implementation, relative causal weight,
health causation, remedy effect, recurrence, shared authority, blame, duty,
liability, receiver work, or external action.

## Event And Scale

Line 132 ruptured in a San Bruno residential area and the released natural gas
ignited. The fire destroyed 38 homes and damaged 70; eight people were killed,
many were injured, and many more were evacuated. Local emergency responders
arrived promptly, while PG&E took 95 minutes to stop the gas flow and isolate
the rupture site.

The event spans pipe fabrication, historical records, pressure and terminal
work, integrity management, SCADA, detection and isolation, emergency
response, operator management, residential standing, state regulation, and
federal oversight without making them one mechanism.
