# SF-0023 - Potomac Midair Airspace, Control, And Safety-Assurance Boundaries

## Boundary

This is a bounded public-source synthesis of the January 29, 2025 midair
collision between PSA Airlines flight 5342 and US Army Priority Air Transport
flight 25 near Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport. It keeps route
design, route altitude and charting, visual separation, controller workload,
radio communication, safety data, FAA and Army safety assurance, collision-
avoidance technology, and affected standing distinct.

real-time authority, data-sharing ownership, Army or FAA disposition,

## Event And Scale

On January 29, 2025, a US Army Sikorsky UH-60L helicopter operating as PAT25
and a PSA Airlines CRJ700 operating as flight 5342 collided about 0.5 miles
southeast of DCA and fell into the Potomac River. The two pilots, two flight
attendants, and 60 passengers aboard the airplane and all three helicopter
crewmembers died. Both aircraft were destroyed.

The event spans route design, published charts, helicopter altitude, aircraft
approach, visual acquisition, air-traffic control, radio transmission,
controller staffing and workload, safety reporting, organizational risk
management, airborne alerting, Army aviation, commercial aviation, and
passenger standing without making them one mechanism.
