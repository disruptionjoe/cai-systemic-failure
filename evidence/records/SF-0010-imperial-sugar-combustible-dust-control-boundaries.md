# SF-0010 - Imperial Sugar Combustible-Dust Control Boundaries

## Boundary

This is a bounded public-source synthesis of the February 7, 2008 sugar-dust
explosion and fire at Imperial Sugar's Port Wentworth refinery. It keeps sugar
release and equipment control, accumulated fuel and housekeeping, conveyor
enclosure and likely ignition, emergency protection, and regulatory standards
distinct.

It does not reconstruct every inspection, management decision, information-
custody path, or person's knowledge; collapse the layers into one mechanism;
determine legal duty or blame beyond the source; establish recurrence or fit
with the provisional review-chain screen; prescribe a remedy; create receiver
work; or request action.

## Event And Scale

The initial explosion occurred in the enclosed steel conveyor beneath the
granulated-sugar silos. Secondary dust explosions and fires propagated through
the packing and loading buildings and parts of the refinery. Fourteen workers
died and dozens were injured. The record spans process equipment, building
fuel accumulation, worker protection, facility management, auditing, and
regulation without treating those levels as one cause.

## System Interaction Lenses

- **Information:** long-known dust hazards and later OSHA emphasis were visible,
  while complete decision-time custody and disposition remain unknown.
- **Resources:** conveying, dust collection, housekeeping, alarms, evacuation,
  and explosion protection are distinct physical and operational resources.
- **Authority:** facility management, insurer assessment, OSHA, and industry
  standards appear as different authority or influence layers; their complete
  decision chain is not reconstructed.
- **Power:** facility design and operating choices shaped worker exposure
  without proving one generalized power mechanism.
- **Participation and standing:** workers carried direct affected standing;
  their full warning, escalation, and correction routes are not reconstructed.
- **Scale:** equipment, building, facility, company, insurer, industry, and
  regulator interact without establishing cross-case recurrence.
- **Feedback and adaptation:** earlier fires, near misses, audits, and hazard
  guidance were available signals; exact closure decisions remain incomplete.

## Domain Absorbers, Counterevidence, And Alternatives

Combustible-dust physics, conveyor and dust-collection design, bearing failure,
housekeeping, hazardous-location electrical practice, building construction,
fire protection, emergency response, occupational safety, insurance, and legal
accounts remain sovereign. The likely bearing ignition, large secondary fuel
load, and inadequate evacuation protections are counterevidence to a single
information-to-authority or review-chain explanation. Specific engineering,
housekeeping, training, and regulatory recommendations remain source-reported
intervention classes, not repository prescriptions or proof of efficacy.
