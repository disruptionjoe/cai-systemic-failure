## Event And Scale

High-pressure natural gas entered a low-pressure distribution system in the
Merrimack Valley, producing fires and explosions. One person died, 22 people
were transported to hospitals, and 131 structures were damaged, including at
least five homes that were destroyed. The low-pressure system was shut down
for 10,894 customers, including precautionary shutoffs outside the immediate
area.

The event spans distribution-system architecture, regulator stations,
engineering, project documentation, construction, inspection, monitoring,
field control, emergency response, operator, contractor, communities, states,
and federal regulation without making them one mechanism.
