# SF-0012 - Lac-Megantic Securement And Oversight Boundaries

## Boundary

This is a bounded public-source synthesis of the July 6, 2013 runaway and
derailment at Lac-Megantic, Quebec. It keeps train securement, locomotive fire
and shutdown, loss of air-brake force, crude-oil classification and tank-car
containment, company safety management, regulatory oversight, and community
consequences distinct.

It does not reconstruct every securement decision, person's knowledge,
information-custody path, or feasible alternative; collapse the layers into
one root cause; determine civil or criminal liability; establish recurrence or
fit with the provisional review-chain screen; prescribe a remedy; create
receiver work; or request action.

## Event And Scale

An unattended Montreal, Maine & Atlantic Railway train rolled about 7.2 miles
from Nantes to Lac-Megantic and reached about 65 mph before derailment. Sixty-
three crude-oil tank cars and two box cars derailed. Forty-seven people died,
about 2,000 people were evacuated, and fires, explosions, released oil, and
environmental contamination caused extensive community harm. The record spans
equipment, train operation, company management, regulation, dangerous-goods
transport, emergency response, and affected-community scales without treating
them as one cause.
