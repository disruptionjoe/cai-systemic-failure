# SF-0014 - Tesoro Anacortes HTHA, Inspection, And Worker Exposure

## Boundary

This is a bounded public-source synthesis of the April 2, 2010 heat-exchanger
rupture, explosion, and fire at the Tesoro Anacortes refinery. It keeps the
physical high-temperature hydrogen attack (HTHA), operating-condition
prediction, inspection limits, material selection, non-routine startup work,
worker exposure, process-hazard analysis, industry guidance, and state
inspection objects distinct.

It does not reconstruct every person's knowledge, inspection, decision, or
information-custody path; collapse the layers into one root cause; determine
blame or legal duty; establish recurrence or fit with the provisional
review-chain screen; prescribe a remedy; create receiver work; or request
action.

## Event And Scale

The in-service E heat exchanger ruptured while workers were returning the
parallel A/B/C exchanger bank to service after cleaning. Hot hydrogen and
naphtha escaped, ignited, and produced an explosion and fire. Seven employees
working nearby were fatally injured. The event spans weld and tubing damage,
heat-exchanger equipment, non-routine unit work, refinery maintenance and
hazard analysis, industry guidance, and state oversight without treating those
scales as one mechanism.
