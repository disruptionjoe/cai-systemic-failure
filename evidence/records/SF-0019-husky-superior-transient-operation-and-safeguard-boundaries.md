# SF-0019 - Husky Superior Transient Operation And Safeguard Boundaries

## Boundary

catalytic cracking unit explosion and subsequent asphalt fire at the Husky
Superior refinery in Wisconsin. It keeps shutdown state, reactor steam

## Event And Scale

During a refinery turnaround shutdown, air entered hydrocarbon-filled FCC
equipment and an explosion occurred in the primary and sponge absorbers.
Debris punctured a hot-asphalt tank; about 17,000 barrels of asphalt spread and
later ignited. Thirty-six workers sought medical attention, the City of
Superior evacuated 2,507 residents, and Duluth issued a shelter-in-place
advisory. The refinery's HF storage tank was not damaged and no HF release
occurred.

The record spans valve, vessel, FCC unit, refinery, turnaround work, emergency
response, neighboring communities, industry knowledge, corporate management,
and regulatory scales without treating them as one mechanism.
