## Event And Scale

Water entered the methyl-mercaptan feed system during a restart problem and,
under cold conditions, formed a hydrate blockage. A technical team directed
hot-water warming and a valve alignment intended to monitor progress. When the
hydrate cleared, liquid methyl mercaptan entered the waste-gas vent header.
Operators interpreted related high pressure as the familiar liquid-
accumulation problem and opened indoor drain valves. Approximately 24,000
pounds of methyl mercaptan were released inside a poorly ventilated
manufacturing building. Four workers died; two other operators who entered in
response to a distress call survived.

The record spans feed piping, vent header, manufacturing building, control
room, technical team, operating shift, emergency-response team, facility
management, workforce, surrounding public, corporate systems, and regulators
without treating them as one mechanism.
