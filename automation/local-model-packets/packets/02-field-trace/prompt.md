# Choose one Upper Big Branch trace opening

Prepare one proposal-only trace-opening decision for the April 2010 Upper Big
Branch mine disaster. Use only the supplied U.S. Mine Safety and Health
Administration facts. Do not retrieve sources. Do not invent testimony,
inspection dates, individual duties, causal weights, legal conclusions,
remedies, or a cross-case pattern.

Source boundary from MSHA's `Fatal Accident Investigation Report, Upper Big
Branch Mine-South`:

- The explosion killed 29 miners and seriously injured two others.
- MSHA concluded that an initial methane ignition transitioned into a massive
  coal-dust explosion; poor roof control, inadequate ventilation, insufficient
  rock dust, and coal-dust accumulations contributed to the physical chain.
- MSHA's root-cause analysis says Performance Coal Company and Massey
  management used practices that produced non-compliance, including miner
  intimidation, illegal advance notice of inspections, inadequate training,
  and failure to maintain required hazard books.
- MSHA says miners were discouraged from reporting or recording hazards, that
  advance notice allowed conditions to be altered before inspectors arrived,
  and that hazards sometimes appeared in production or maintenance records
  but not in required examination books.
- This packet does not supply the complete report, full inspection chronology,
  every alternative, individual accountability, counterevidence, or evidence
  for a remedy or recurrence claim.

Return exactly nine short lines:

- **Unit:** exactly `Upper Big Branch Mine-South operating and safety-control
  system`.
- **Period:** exactly `conditions preceding April 5, 2010`.
- **Affected standing:** exactly `29 miners killed and two seriously injured`.
- **Trace route:** choose exactly one of `hazard-recording-to-correction`,
  `inspection-visibility`, `miner-standing`, or `physical-propagation`.
- **Source support:** name two supplied MSHA facts that directly support the
  chosen route.
- **Boundary:** name one nearby supplied route that must remain separate.
- **Alternative:** name one narrower explanation still possible without the
  missing chronology and counterevidence.
- **Unknown:** name one missing source-local link required to complete the
  chosen trace.
- **Opening decision:** choose exactly `open_trace` or `defer_for_source_gap`.

An `open_trace` decision opens only a source-verification candidate for later
Frontier review. Attribute MSHA findings to MSHA; do not create an accepted
record, causal allocation, recurrence, schema pressure, independent blame
finding, remedy, receiver work, or external-action request. Use plain
Markdown, no table or frontmatter, and keep it under 240 words.
