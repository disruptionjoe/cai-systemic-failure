- On March 26, 2024, the 984-foot containership Dali lost electrical power,
  propulsion, and steering near Baltimore's Francis Scott Key Bridge, struck

  improper wire-label-banding installation, caused a breaker to open and

  to collapse and loss of life; such measures could have followed a
  vulnerability assessment recommended by AASHTO.
- Quick pilot, dispatcher, and Maryland Transportation Authority actions
  stopped bridge traffic and prevented greater loss of life, but ineffective
  immediate communication left highway workers without evacuation notice.
- Six highway workers died; one survived with serious injuries, the inspector
  escaped, and one person aboard Dali had a minor injury. Keep worker, road,
  vessel, port, and community standing distinct.
