  `https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/AccidentReports/Reports/RIR2405%20CORRECTED.pdf`.
- NTSB news release `NR20240625`, *Failed Wheel Bearing Caused Norfolk Southern
  Train Derailment in East Palestine, Ohio*, is the compact official summary:
  `https://www.ntsb.gov/news/press-releases/Pages/NR20240625.aspx`.
- The NTSB found that a defective wheel bearing failed and overheated, causing
  the February 3, 2023 derailment and hazardous-material release. Wayside
  monitoring did not diagnose the hot bearing in time for mitigation before
  derailment; a critical alarm occurred only shortly before the derailment.
- Eleven tank cars carrying hazardous materials derailed. Keep tank-car damage,
  fire exposure, material release, crashworthiness, placarding, and hazardous-
  materials rules inside their sovereign technical and regulatory domains.
- Three days later, the local incident commander approved venting and burning
  five vinyl-chloride-monomer tank cars. The NTSB found that decision
  unnecessary and based on incomplete and misleading information supplied by
  Norfolk Southern officials and contractors. The incident commander retained
  on-scene decision authority; do not convert reliance on supplied information
  into proof that another actor held that authority.
- The NTSB separately identified lack of accurate, timely, and comprehensive
  information to local incident commanders and state officials, emergency-
  responder training limits, burned-away hazardous-material placards, wayside-
  monitoring limits, and continued DOT-111 tank-car use as safety issues.
- The record spans freight-rail operations, hazardous-materials transport,
  local emergency response, state information needs, and an affected community.
  Those layers are connected by the event but are not one proven recurring
