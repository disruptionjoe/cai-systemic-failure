Supplied facts from CSB `2015-02-I-CA` and its source-local comparison in CSB

- On February 18, 2015, an explosion occurred in the ExxonMobil Torrance
  refinery FCC unit's electrostatic precipitator, a pollution-control device.
  Two workers suffered minor injuries.
- The FCC unit was in an idled mode while workers prepared to isolate
  equipment for unscheduled maintenance.
- Preparations caused a pressure deviation that disturbed the reactor steam
  barrier. Flammable hydrocarbon backflowed from the main column through the
  reactor and regenerator into the flue-gas system.
- Hydrocarbon mixed with air and ignited inside the electrostatic
  precipitator. This flow direction differs from Husky Superior's later air-
  into-hydrocarbon path.

  effect at another facility.
