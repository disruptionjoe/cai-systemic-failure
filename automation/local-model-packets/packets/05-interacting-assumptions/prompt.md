# SF Schema Candidate 0001 - Review-Chain Fields

## Boundary

This is a provisional review-chain field schema candidate for source-backed
failure records. It implements the acceptance gate from
`SF-SCHEMA-READINESS-0001-review-chain-fields.md`.

It is not an accepted topology schema, universal systemic-failure ontology,
domain remedy, public-health recommendation, engineering recommendation,
legal or civil-rights finding, CAI doctrine, intervention plan, or proving
authority. It is a candidate structure to be falsified, narrowed, or revised
by a fourth source-backed record or negative absorber.

## Record Qualification Gate

A record may use this candidate only when all gate conditions are met:

1. source material is public, licensed, safely shareable, or synthetic;
2. the record names the source context and relied-on revision, preserves any
   known materially superseding or contradicting revision in the admitted
   source packet, preserves and grades any direct material contradiction within
   that packet that bears on a required field, and keeps correction routes
   open;
3. an old assumption is reused under a changed condition or changed evidence
   context;
4. the changed condition creates a validation burden that sources show as
   unmet, disputed, fragmented, or not owner-visible enough;
5. affected system or affected-party standing remains explicit;
6. delegation, review, or request paths are material to the contradiction; and
7. at least one narrower absorber or countermodel is recorded.

If these conditions are not met, the case may still be useful evidence, but it
does not qualify as positive evidence for this candidate.

## Candidate Fields

| Field | Required content | Refusal condition |
|---|---|---|
| `assumption_source_context` | Name the reused assumption and the source, report, decision context, revision, version, date, or evidence basis relied on for the synthesis. Where multiple fragments support a record, name the admitted source-backed bridge connecting the qualifying assumption, changed condition, standing, and review path to one bounded system or review chain. Preserve and disposition any known later revision, or direct material contradiction, in the admitted source packet that supersedes, narrows, withdraws, or conflicts with that support. | Refuse if the record only says a system failed without tying the assumption to source context, combines unrelated source fragments without an admitted evidentiary bridge, knowingly selects an earlier supporting revision while omitting a later material contradiction, or labels a directly contradicted required field passed without grading the conflict. |

| `affected_system_and_standing` | Name the bounded affected system and the affected-party standing visible in the sources, including people when sources make them central; when assembled from multiple fragments, preserve the admitted bridge that connects their standing to that system. | Refuse if affected parties disappear behind process language or the record borrows standing from an unrelated system without a source-backed bridge. |

This falsifier tests whether individually traceable fragments can qualify as one review-chain record when the admitted synthetic packet provides no source-backed bridge showing that the assumption, changed condition, affected standing, and accountable review path belong to the same bounded system.

It uses only a safely shareable synthetic fixture and repository controls. It does not assert a real-world failure, source, organization, relationship, or remedy; accept a schema; authorize research or participation; or move another owner's work.

## Falsifier Class

The candidate requires named sources, affected standing, review ownership, and other field-level evidence. Those controls can still be gamed by assembling plausible fragments from unrelated contexts: one fragment names an inherited assumption in System A, another a changed condition in System B, and another an affected-party or review path in System C. Each fragment is traceable, but their juxtaposition does not establish a bounded failure record.

The candidate need not demand a single source, identical wording, or exhaustive proof of every relationship. It must require the admitted packet to show a source-backed connection, or explicitly grade the absence of one, before treating fragments as one positively qualifying review chain.

## Synthetic Fixture

The abstract fixture supplies apparent field-level fit:

- a named synthetic source fragment describes an old operating assumption in System A;
- a separate named fragment describes a changed operating condition in System B;
- a third fragment describes affected-party standing and a review address in System C;
- each fragment is safely shareable, revision-labeled, and carries an explicit synthetic basis;
- the packet offers an inspectable countermodel and a route-capability premise; but
- no fragment or admitted bridge identifies A, B, and C as the same bounded affected system or connected review chain, while the record labels every gate passed.

No real institution, source packet, person, or failure is represented.

## Verdict

`SF-SCHEMA-CANDIDATE-0001` refuses this fixture as positive evidence. A record may draw on multiple sources, revisions, or perspectives, but its admitted packet must identify the source-backed connection that makes the required fields evidence about one bounded affected system and review chain. When that connection is unavailable, preserve the fragments as separate leads, uncertainty, absorbers, or topology questions rather than manufacturing a combined record.

## Candidate Revision Pressure

Keep the nine core fields and provisional status unchanged. Narrow only the existing source-context and affected-system requirements: source context must state the admitted evidence bridge, where multiple fragments are used, that connects the qualifying assumption, changed condition, standing, and review path to the bounded record. Refuse positive qualification when the record combines unrelated fragments without that bridge. This does not require one source, exhaustiveness, or certainty; a contested or incomplete bridge remains explicitly graded rather than silently passed.

## Non-Promotion Result

This falsifier does not accept the candidate or create an ontology, a real-world record, a remedy, a recommendation, research, participation, contact, posting outside GitHub versioning, deployment, spending, account access, CAI phase or relationship movement, or authority over another owner.
