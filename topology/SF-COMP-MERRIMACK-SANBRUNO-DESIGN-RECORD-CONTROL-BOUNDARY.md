# Merrimack / San Bruno Design-Record-Control Boundary

## Boundary

This comparison preserves a bounded separation between the Merrimack Valley
distribution-project control sequence in `NTSB/PAR-19/02` and the San Bruno
transmission-pipeline fabrication, record, and integrity-management sequence in
`NTSB/PAR-11/01`.

It does not infer recurrence, one design-record-control mechanism, complete
custody, shared authority, relative causal weight, schema pressure, blame,
duty, remedy, acceptance, receiver work, liability, health causation, or
external action.

## Decision, Counterevidence, And Falsifier

Decision: `preserve_separate_project_and_integrity_control_phases`.

The records support a bounded comparison at the information-and-review level,
while their physical systems, decision times, implementation loci, detection
objects, and authority surfaces remain materially different. Those differences
are counterevidence to one recurring design-record-control mechanism.

Withdraw or materially narrow this comparison if closer official-source review
shows that the Merrimack work package did not omit the sensing-line
configuration, or that San Bruno records did not misidentify the seam state and
the integrity program did not use an assessment method unable to detect the
seam-weld defect. Strengthen no relation without source-local custody,
authority, disposition, implementation, and control-effect evidence for both
cases.
