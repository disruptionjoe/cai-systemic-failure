## Decision, Counterevidence, And Falsifier

Decision: `preserve_separate_maintenance_operation_boundaries`.

The two reports support only a broad pressure in which maintenance-related
objects connect to later operation and oversight. Installed-component
lubrication, interval setting, inspection opportunity, progressive wear, and
trim-system failure remain materially different from removed-component
handling, physical safing, work-card signoff, hazardous-material packaging,
cargo loading, and cargo fire. Those distinctions, together with different
crew responses, design conditions, program relations, and FAA roles, are
counterevidence to recurrence or one cross-case mechanism.

Falsify or materially narrow this comparison if closer official-source review
shows that `NTSB/AAR-02/01` does not connect insufficient lubrication and
extended intervals to the jackscrew failure, or that `NTSB/AAR-97/06` does not
connect uncapped removed generators, work-card completion, packaging and
loading with the cargo fire. Removal of either source-local side defeats the
selected bounded two-record comparison. Reject a stronger comparison that
fills incomplete custody, decision, approval, surveillance, correction,
implementation, relative-causal-weight, or verified-control-effect links.
