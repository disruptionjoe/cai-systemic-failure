# Contradiction-Visibility Candidate Disposition

## Boundary

This disposition closes the one-cycle diagnostic exception over the Boeing,
Challenger, Columbia, and Flint pressure artifacts. It does not reopen sources,
establish recurrence, infer custody or authority, merge domain explanations,
accept schema, prescribe a remedy, create receiver work, or request action.

## Candidate Under Test

The bounded candidate says that each case independently preserves decision-
relevant contradiction evidence that was not kept fully visible as one
accountable review object. The cases do not establish a shared custody,
authority, disposition, stop, validation method, or causal mechanism.

## Decision Seam

- **Evidence object:** each case has its own source-bounded contradiction or
  evidence object; no object, custody path, or fact transfers across cases.
- **Affected standing:** affected standing is material inside each owner record
  but does not create one cross-case decision or authority relation.
- **Decision-time authority:** complete recipient, authority, timing,
  disposition, and stop chains remain `unknown` across the candidate.
- **Domain absorber:** aviation certification, spaceflight, drinking-water,
  public-health, engineering, regulatory, legal, and public-safety explanations
  remain sovereign.
- **Operative falsifier:** source-preserved application of the current screen
  must reveal a decision-relevant contradiction relation that its existing
  fields cannot represent without losing a material distinction.

## Disposition

`absorbed_by_existing_screen`

## Novel Residue

No independently discriminating residue is established. Contradiction
visibility is already expressible through `observation_environment`,
`delegation_visibility_gap`, `validation_burden`, affected standing,
absorber/counterevidence, and correction-route or stop-condition controls.

## Existing-Screen Absorber

The provisional screen can preserve the evidence object, affected standing,
distributed visibility, validation pressure, domain alternatives, and an
unresolved correction or stop route without adding a new field or inquiry.
That fit does not accept the screen or turn the four cases into positives.

## Unknowns

Complete custody, recipient transitions, decision-time authority, timing,
disposition, accountable stop, validation method, and one shared causal
mechanism remain unknown.

## Falsifier

application across at least two cases identifies the same bounded
contradiction relation, including accountable recipient, decision-time
authority, disposition or stop, and causal relevance, while showing exactly
which material distinction the existing screen cannot carry. Narrow or retire
the candidate if closer review shows that its residue depends on case count,
shared domain, severity, institutional plurality, later investigation, or
borrowed facts.
