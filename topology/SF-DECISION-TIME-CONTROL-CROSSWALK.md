# SF Decision-Time Control Crosswalk

## Boundary

This provisional comparison keeps five synthetic controls independently usable.
It adds no field or record, accepts no schema, infers no authority exercise or
outcome, prescribes no remedy, requests no action, and performs no external
action. Passing or failing one control does not decide another.

## Control Crosswalk

| Control | Decision object | Refusal condition | Evidence preserved after refusal | Not equivalent to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `SF-TC-0001` | Assumption operative reuse | The assumption was retired before the changed condition and no continued-reliance or reuse bridge is admitted. | Later chronology remains graded. | Owner authority, evidence handling, validation timing, or correction-route stop. |
| `SF-DRA-0001` | Decision-time relevant owner authority | Relevant authority begins only after the reuse decision is final. | Later authority remains graded historical or future-route evidence. | Assumption reuse, evidence handling, validation timing, or correction-route stop. |
| `SF-ORC-0001` | Pre-decision evidence handling | Routing or dispute occurs only after the reuse decision but is merged into a timeless pass. | Later handling remains graded historical, route, or unresolved evidence. | Assumption reuse, owner authority, validation timing, or correction-route stop. |
| `SF-VBT-0001` | Decision-time validation-burden satisfaction | Validation completes only after the reuse decision and is backdated to mark the decision-time burden satisfied. | Later validation remains graded historical or follow-on evidence. | Assumption reuse, owner authority, evidence handling, or correction-route stop. |
| `SF-CSC-0001` | Backed qualification stop while contradiction remains unresolved | A capable route has no explicit source- or synthetic-backed stop condition. | Route capability remains graded without qualifying the record. | Assumption reuse, owner authority, evidence handling, or validation timing. |

## Non-Collapse Checks

- `SF-TC-0001` asks whether the assumption remained operative, not who had
  authority, when evidence or validation arrived, or whether a stop condition
  existed.
- `SF-DRA-0001` asks who held relevant authority during the decision window;
  that cannot establish reuse, timely handling or validation, or a stop.
- `SF-ORC-0001` asks when evidence was handled; visibility or routing cannot
  establish operative reuse, authority, validation satisfaction, or a stop.
- `SF-VBT-0001` asks whether the validation burden was satisfied in the
  relevant decision window; later completion cannot decide the other controls.
- `SF-CSC-0001` asks whether unresolved contradiction has a backed
  qualification stop; route capability cannot decide the other controls.

## Combined Use

One record may implicate several controls, but each decision object is evaluated
separately against admitted evidence. The crosswalk creates no hierarchy,
universal sequence, accepted schema, or inference that any control was
exercised or caused an outcome.

## Candidate Effect

Comparison artifact only. The nine fields, five positive records, provisional
status, acceptance boundary, and no-remedy rule remain unchanged.
