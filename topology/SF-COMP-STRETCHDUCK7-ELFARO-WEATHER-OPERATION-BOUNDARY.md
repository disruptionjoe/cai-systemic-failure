## Decision, Counterevidence, And Falsifier

Decision: `preserve_bounded_weather_to_operation_comparison`.

The shared pressure is limited to official findings that connect weather
information and later operation within each case. The short lake tour versus
ocean voyage, manager-led sequencing versus captain and bridge route authority,
different vessel states, different flooding and survival paths, and different
standing and oversight relations are counterevidence to recurrence or one
cross-case mechanism.

Falsify or materially narrow this comparison if closer official-source review
shows that either report does not connect available weather information with
the named operating decision. Reject a stronger comparison if it requires
filling the incomplete information custody, decision chronology, challenge,
authority, vessel-state custody, correction, implementation, relative causal
weight, or verified control-effect links.
