# SF-SRL-0001 - Source-Revision Lineage

## Boundary

This falsifier tests whether `SF-SCHEMA-CANDIDATE-0001` can satisfy its source
control by naming a source revision that appears to support positive
qualification while omitting a known later revision that withdraws or
materially contradicts that support.

It uses a synthetic two-revision fixture and the repository's existing
candidate and records. It does not claim that any real source was revised, add
a real-world failure record, accept a topology schema, create a universal
systemic-failure ontology, prescribe a remedy, issue a public recommendation,
authorize human or field research, or move another owner's work.

No confidential material, human subjects, field work, account access, external
contact, posting, deployment, spending, publication outside normal GitHub
versioning, or external action was used.

## Falsifier Class

The candidate requires `assumption_source_context` and repository governance
requires named sources and revisions. Those controls make a citation
inspectable, but they do not yet state what happens when the relied-on revision
has a known material successor.

That omission permits revision laundering. A record could cite revision 1,
which supports an inherited-assumption synthesis, while a known revision 2
withdraws the relevant passage, corrects the underlying evidence, or states
that the synthesis is not supportable. The citation would be real and
revision-specific, but the record would hide the source lineage needed for a
correctable claim.

Not every later revision defeats an earlier one. Editorial, formatting, or
unrelated changes do not alter qualification. The pressure exists only when a
known later revision materially changes the source support carrying a required
candidate field.

## Synthetic Fixture

The fixture is intentionally abstract:

- a safely shareable packet has two explicitly labeled revisions;
- revision 1 states facts that appear to support an inherited operating
  assumption, a changed condition, and an unmet validation burden;
- revision 2 is known to the record author and materially withdraws or
  contradicts the revision-1 support for the inherited-assumption synthesis;
- the record cites revision 1, names the remaining candidate fields as fixture
  premises, and does not disclose revision 2;
- no explanation shows that revision 1 remains authoritative for the relied-on
  claim, and no unresolved conflict grade is recorded; and
- the record nevertheless labels the source gate "passed."

This fixture is not evidence that any actual report, source, or system followed
this revision history. It isolates whether a traceable citation can still
misrepresent source support when material lineage is omitted.

## Verdict

`SF-SCHEMA-CANDIDATE-0001` refuses this fixture as positive evidence.

A superseded revision may remain part of the historical evidence chain. It
cannot alone carry current positive qualification when a known later revision
materially withdraws or contradicts the support on which qualification
depends. The record must identify the relied-on revision, preserve the material
lineage, and state whether the later revision supersedes, narrows,
contradicts, or leaves the source support unresolved.

This refusal does not require the repository to prove that no later revision
exists, monitor external sources, or treat every newer version as controlling.
It applies to material revision conflict already known within the admitted
source packet.

## Candidate Revision Pressure

Keep the frozen core field list and candidate status unchanged. Narrow the
existing source controls so:

- the record identifies the source revision, version, date, or equivalent
  relied on for the qualifying synthesis;
- a known later revision that materially supersedes, narrows, withdraws, or
  contradicts that support is preserved and dispositioned;
- an earlier revision may remain historical evidence but cannot silently carry
  current qualification after its material support is withdrawn; and
- unresolved revision conflict remains explicitly graded uncertainty rather
  than a passed source gate.

Refuse positive qualification when the record knowingly selects an earlier
supporting revision and omits a later material contradiction. Do not refuse
merely because a later revision exists or because it changes unrelated content.
