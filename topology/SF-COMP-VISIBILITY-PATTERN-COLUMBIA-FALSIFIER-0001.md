## Disposition

Columbia does not falsify the narrow visibility residue: its owner record
independently says the relevant concern, evidence need, dissent, disposition,
and standing were not preserved as one accountable contradiction object. Three
cases still do not establish recurrence, and no shared custody, authority,
disposition, or stop mechanism is proved.

contradiction object, custody, accountable recipient and authority, decision-
