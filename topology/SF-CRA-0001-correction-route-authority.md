# SF-CRA-0001 - Correction-Route Authority

## Boundary

This falsifier tests whether `SF-SCHEMA-CANDIDATE-0001` can qualify a record
that names a visible correction route but whose admitted synthetic packet shows
the route cannot receive, review, escalate, stop, revise, or expose the
assumption at issue.

It uses only a synthetic fixture and existing repository controls. It does not
assert a real-world failure, correction path, authority relationship, or remedy;
accept a topology schema; create an ontology; authorize research or
participation; or move another owner's work.

## Falsifier Class

`SF-CRT-0001` already refuses a fixture with no named correction route. That
does not establish that every named route is capable of correcting the record.
A route label can create a false appearance of accountability when the packet
also shows it has no relevant remit, access to the contradiction, authority to
escalate it, or means to make the assumption reviewable.

The pressure is narrow: the candidate should require the record to show that
the named route is owner-visible *and* connected to a stated correction,
escalation, stop, revision, or exposure capability. It need not prove a route
will succeed, prescribe how an owner should act, or infer authority beyond the
admitted packet.

## Synthetic Fixture

The abstract fixture supplies all apparent positive-fit premises:

- a safely shareable synthetic packet identifies an old sourced assumption and
  a changed condition;
- the packet states a source-backed synthetic validation burden, affected
  standing, review fragmentation, and an inspectable countermodel;
- the record names a public-facing escalation desk as its correction route;
- the same packet explicitly limits that desk to logging general feedback: it
  cannot receive the underlying evidence, review the assumption, reach the
  accountable review owner, or escalate, stop, revise, or expose the relevant
  claim; and
- despite that limitation, the record labels the correction-route gate passed.

No real institution, route, authority, or failure is represented by this
fixture.

## Verdict

`SF-SCHEMA-CANDIDATE-0001` refuses this fixture as positive evidence. A record
must name a route and state, from the admitted source or explicit synthetic
basis, how that route can receive or surface the relevant contradiction to a
party able to correct, escalate, stop, revise, or expose the assumption.

This is not a success guarantee. A route may be imperfect, contested, delayed,
or unresolved and still be recorded with explicit uncertainty. The refusal
applies when the record itself shows that its named route cannot carry the
relevant correction and nonetheless treats the gate as passed.

## Candidate Revision Pressure

Keep the nine core fields and provisional status unchanged. Narrow only
`correction_route_stop_condition`: refuse positive qualification when a named
route is merely a label or when admitted material shows it cannot receive,
review, escalate, stop, revise, or expose the relevant assumption through an
accountable path. Preserve such a case as an unresolved inquiry, absorber, or
separate topology question rather than inventing a route or remedy.
