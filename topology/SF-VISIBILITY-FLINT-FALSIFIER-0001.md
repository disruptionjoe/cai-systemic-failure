## Boundary

This gate tests only whether the three-case aerospace visibility candidate
survives pressure from the bounded Flint owner record. It does not merge cases,
establish recurrence, infer custody or authority, assign blame or duty,
prescribe a water or aerospace remedy, accept schema, create receiver work, or
request action.

## Aerospace Candidate

`SF-COMP-VISIBILITY-PATTERN-COLUMBIA-FALSIFIER-0001` preserves a three-case
aerospace candidate: each bounded record independently retains decision-

## Flint Pressure

`SF-0003` independently preserves a changed water source, corrosion-control
and exposure validation burdens, resident complaints, independent sampling,

unknown.

## Decision Seam

- **Evidence object:** Flint's changed-source, complaint, sampling, and
  official-concern evidence is independently source-bounded; no aerospace fact
  is borrowed.
- **Affected standing:** residents carried the exposure risk while technical
  and regulatory status remained disputed.
- **Decision-time authority:** complete utility, state, federal, health, and
  emergency authority transitions remain unknown.
- **Domain absorber:** drinking-water treatment, sampling, public health, civil
  rights, oversight, emergency authority, and legal explanations remain
  sovereign.
- **Operative falsifier:** narrow this result to `aerospace_only_candidate` if
  closer owner or source review shows Flint's contradiction was timely held as
  one accountable object, or mark it `refuted` if the apparent relation depends
  only on shared harm, institutional plurality, delay, or later investigation.

evidence. Flint concerns drinking-water operations, sampling, public health,
