# Alaska Airlines Flight 261 / American Airlines Flight 587 Aircraft-Control-Loss Boundary

## Boundary

This comparison preserves only a bounded aircraft-control-loss contrast
supported by `NTSB/AAR-02/01`, `NTSB/AAR-04/04`, `SF-0032`, and `SF-0034`.
Both events ended in loss of aircraft control, but one source-local chain runs
from in-service lubrication and inspection through jackscrew wear and trim-
system failure while the other runs from a wake encounter and pilot inputs
through rudder response, aerodynamic load, and vertical-stabilizer separation.
