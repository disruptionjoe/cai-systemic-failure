# SF-IPC-0001 - Intra-Packet Contradiction

## Boundary

This falsifier tests whether `SF-SCHEMA-CANDIDATE-0001` can qualify a record
when its admitted synthetic packet directly and materially contradicts the
source support for a required positive-fit field. It uses only a safely
shareable synthetic fixture and repository controls. It does not assert a
real-world source, contradiction, failure, organization, or remedy; accept a
schema; authorize research or participation; or move another owner's work.

## Falsifier Class

The candidate rejects source-thin reconstruction, omitted known later material
revisions, weak alternatives, and unbridged source composition. Those controls
do not explicitly resolve a direct contradiction already visible in the
admitted packet: a record can accurately name its source and revision, yet
present one excerpt as support while leaving a materially conflicting excerpt
undispositioned.

This is not a demand for source unanimity. A contradiction may narrow a claim,
leave it unresolved, or establish a bounded qualification with a stated basis.
The false-positive path exists only when the record labels a required field
passed while failing to grade a direct material conflict that the admitted
packet itself makes visible.

## Synthetic Fixture

The abstract fixture supplies apparent field-level fit:

- a labeled synthetic packet states that an inherited operating assumption was
  relied on under a changed condition;
- a second labeled excerpt in the same packet directly states that the assumed
  condition was not relied on, or that the claimed changed condition did not
  apply to the bounded record;
- the conflict is material to `assumption_source_context`, not merely an
  editorial difference or a separate domain interpretation;
- the remaining candidate fields, a traceable countermodel, and a capable
  correction-route premise are supplied as fixture premises; and
- the record names the packet revision, quotes only the supporting excerpt,
  labels the source gate passed, and neither preserves nor grades the direct
  contradiction.

No real institution, person, evidence packet, or failure is represented.

## Verdict

`SF-SCHEMA-CANDIDATE-0001` refuses this fixture as positive evidence. A record
may preserve conflicting source material, but it must identify and grade a
direct material contradiction in its admitted packet before claiming that the
affected required field passes. The case may remain historical, narrowed,
contradicted, unresolved, an absorber, or a separate topology question.

This refusal does not require the repository to settle source disputes, prove
that all sources agree, or treat every tension as disqualifying. It applies
only to a direct, material conflict already visible within the admitted packet
for a required qualifying field.

## Candidate Revision Pressure

Keep the nine core fields and provisional status unchanged. Narrow the
existing `assumption_source_context` and `absorber_or_counterevidence` controls
so a record preserves and grades any direct material contradiction within its
admitted packet that bears on a required field. Refuse positive qualification
when the record presents one side as passed while leaving that conflict
undispositioned. Preserve nonmaterial variation, contested evidence, and
unresolved contradictions with explicit grades rather than erasing them.
