# Husky Superior / Torrance FCC Transient-Operation Boundary

## Boundary

This comparison tests only a bounded relation between two fluid catalytic
cracking incidents during transient operating states. Husky Superior involved
air moving from the regenerator side into hydrocarbon-filled equipment during
turnaround shutdown. Torrance involved main-column hydrocarbons moving through
the reactor and regenerator toward an energized electrostatic precipitator
while the unit was in Safe Park for maintenance-isolation work.

The comparison does not equate the incidents; infer recurrence, one root
cause, a shared maintenance, authority, management, or remedy mechanism;
accept a schema; assign blame or duty; create receiver work; make an HF,
exposure, or health conclusion; or authorize external action.

## Decision, Counterevidence, And Falsifier

Decision: `bounded_opposite_direction_transient_relation`.

The incidents support a bounded relation at the transient-operation and
air-hydrocarbon-separation level: both required steam and mechanical barrier
conditions to keep air and hydrocarbons apart while the FCC unit was outside
normal operation. Their shutdown and Safe Park states, purge and pressure
conditions, opposite flow directions, ignition locations, escalation paths,
standing, and learning surfaces are counterevidence to one shared causal,
maintenance, authority, management, or remedy mechanism.

Withdraw the bounded relation if closer official-source review shows that
either incident did not occur during a transient FCC state or did not involve
loss of the described air-hydrocarbon separation through steam and slide-valve
conditions. Narrow either side if source review changes a named state, flow,
safeguard, consequence, standing, or industry-learning fact. Strengthen no
mechanism claim without complete source-local knowledge, custody, authority,
disposition, implementation, relative-causal-weight, exposure, and control-
effect chains for both incidents.
