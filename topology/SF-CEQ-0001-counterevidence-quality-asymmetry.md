# SF-CEQ-0001 - Counterevidence-Quality Asymmetry

## Boundary

This falsifier tests whether `SF-SCHEMA-CANDIDATE-0001` can satisfy its
counterevidence control merely by naming a generic alternative that has no
traceable source context or explicit claim posture.

It uses a synthetic fixture and the repository's existing candidate and
records. It does not add a real-world failure record, accept a topology schema,
create a universal systemic-failure ontology, prescribe a remedy, issue a
public recommendation, authorize human or field research, or move another
owner's work.

No confidential material, human subjects, field work, account access, external
contact, posting, deployment, spending, publication outside normal GitHub
versioning, or external action was used.

## Falsifier Class

The candidate requires `absorber_or_counterevidence` so that a plausible
review-chain fit cannot count every case as confirmation. The current field
requires the strongest narrower explanation and its disposition, but does not
explicitly require the alternative's source context or claim posture.

That omission permits asymmetric evidence quality. A record could carefully
source the apparent positive fit, append "operator error, resource scarcity, or
technical defect may explain the failure," and appear counterevidence-aware
without exposing whether the alternative is supported, synthetic, source-thin,
contradicted, or unresolved.

Counterevidence does not need equal source volume or certainty. It does need
enough traceability and grading for a reader to tell whether it can actually
absorb, narrow, or fail to kill the candidate.

## Synthetic Fixture

The fixture is intentionally abstract:

- a safely shareable packet identifies an inherited operating assumption, a
  changed condition, a source-backed validation burden, affected standing, a
  fragmented review path, and an owner-visible correction route;
- the apparent review-chain fit is traceable to named packet sections;
- the record's only absorber statement says that operator error, resource
  scarcity, or a technical defect could instead explain the failure;
- no source context, synthetic premise, evidence pointer, claim posture, or
  condition for testing those alternatives is named; and
- the record nevertheless labels the counterevidence gate "passed."

This fixture is not evidence that any real system failed. It tests whether a
generic alternative can perform the appearance of contradiction without
carrying inspectable counterevidence.

## Verdict

`SF-SCHEMA-CANDIDATE-0001` refuses this fixture as positive evidence.

The result identifies a narrow quality asymmetry: source-backed positive-fit
fields plus an untraceable alternative do not satisfy the counterevidence gate.
The alternative may remain a lead or unresolved possibility, but the record
cannot claim candidate qualification until it states the alternative's source
context or synthetic basis, grades its claim posture, and explains what effect
it has on the review-chain residue.

This refusal does not require equal evidence on every side and does not treat
missing counterevidence as evidence that the candidate is true.

## Candidate Revision Pressure

Keep the frozen core field list and candidate status unchanged. Narrow the
existing `absorber_or_counterevidence` field so required content includes:

- the strongest narrower explanation;
- its source context or explicit synthetic basis;
- its claim posture, such as supported, source-thin, contradicted, synthetic,
  or unresolved; and
- whether it absorbs, narrows, or fails to kill the review-chain residue.

Refuse positive qualification when the record names only generic,
untraceable, or ungraded alternatives. Preserve them as leads or uncertainties
instead of silently upgrading them into counterevidence.
