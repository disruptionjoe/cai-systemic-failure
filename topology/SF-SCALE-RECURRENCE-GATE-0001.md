# Cross-Scale Recurrence Gate For The Partial Flint And Texas Traces

## Boundary

This gate compares only the two completed official-report trace reviews. It
does not complete an unsupported link, reopen the deferred Texas hypothesis,
equate municipal water/federal oversight with regional electric/gas systems,
infer blame or duty, prescribe a remedy, accept a schema, create receiver
work, or request action.

## Cases Under Test

The Flint review preserves a partial complaint-to-institution trace. The Texas
review preserves a partial prior-knowledge-to-readiness trace. Both are
provisional source-review results; neither is a complete decision-time chain.

## Scale Gate

| Dimension | Flint EPA-OIG trace | Texas FERC/NERC trace |
|---|---|---|
| system scale | municipal drinking-water system with state and federal oversight | regional electric system with gas-electric interdependence |
| evidence object | dated complaints, an elevated-lead result, and a sampling-protocol concern | dated 2011 joint-report knowledge object |
| source | EPA OIG analysis of Region 5 records | 2021 FERC/NERC/Regional Entity final report |
| custody | `unknown` for one selected observation | `unknown` for the 2011 knowledge object |
| accountable authority | `unknown` for one selected observation and authority then applied | `unknown` for one accountable recipient and authority then in force |
| validation burden | `unknown` for one observation's linkage to a then-current validation object | supported, limited translation into recurring readiness-check inputs |
| disposition / stop | `unknown` source-preserved disposition and timely unresolved stop | `unknown` source-linked decision and accountable stop or closure state |
| causal relevance | `unknown` for one bounded decision or outcome | supported, limited source-stated relevance; no complete causal chain |
| domain absorber | water treatment, health, civil rights, regulatory oversight, and legal explanations | electric and gas systems, equipment, fuel, planning, operating, resource adequacy, regulatory, and legal explanations |

## Disposition

`insufficient_for_scale_claim`.

The two partial traces do not supply one repeated mechanism across scales.
They show that a public-source evidence object can enter an institutional
record while load-bearing custody, authority, decision, stop, or causal links
remain incomplete. That is an evidence-state similarity, not a systemic-
failure recurrence result.

## Shared Residue

Both reviews preserve a dated evidence object, an official source, material
unknowns, and a refusal to infer a complete trace. Flint supports institutional
receipt but no validation link; Texas supports limited validation translation
and relevance but no accountable decision or closure. Those differences
prevent one shared decision-time chain.

## Scope Refused

This gate refuses cross-scale recurrence, a shared review-chain mechanism,
case-count inference, severity inference, borrowed links, reopening the Texas
hypothesis, a sixth positive record, schema pressure, remedy, receiver work,
and external action.

## Evidence Required

Before revisiting recurrence, reconstruct each case independently with source-
local evidence for its evidence object, custody, accountable recipient and
authority, then-current validation burden, disposition, timely unresolved stop
or closure, and causal relevance. Only then compare a named common mechanism;
one completed case cannot fill the other's missing links.

## Falsifier

Revise this negative gate if independently complete, source-preserving traces
at the two scales support the same bounded mechanism while retaining their
different evidence types, authority topologies, timing, and domain absorbers.
Mark the recurrence hypothesis `refuted`, rather than merely insufficient, if
complete evidence establishes materially incompatible mechanisms or timely
adequate disposition in the decision objects under comparison.

## Candidate Effect

TEST CROSS-SCALE RECURRENCE USING TWO PARTIAL PUBLIC-SOURCE TRACES — NO SCHEMA, REMEDY, ACCEPTANCE, RECEIVER-WORK, OR EXTERNAL-ACTION CHANGE

Frontier retained the candidate's `insufficient_for_scale_claim` result,
