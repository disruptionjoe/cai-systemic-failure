# Public Stress-Knowledge Hypothesis Disposition

## Boundary

This disposition uses only the completed Texas/California, Texas/Fukushima,
and Texas/I-35W comparisons. It does not convert a case into schema evidence,
merge domain causes, infer blame or legal duty, prescribe a remedy, accept a
schema, create receiver work, or request action.

## Comparison Evidence

Texas/California supports only that extreme weather exposed a gap between
stressed conditions and dependable electricity resources; California remains
a resource-adequacy absorber. Texas/Fukushima preserves prior stress attention
in unlike electric/gas and nuclear-hazard systems, but the common decision-
time knowledge-to-validation chain remains unproved. Texas/I-35W shows that
prior attention, changed stress or load, many actors, severe outcome, and later
correction routes do not establish one mechanism; I-35W remains absorbed by
structural capacity, original design calculation, rating, inspection,
construction load, and guidance.

## Supported Residue

The strongest surviving residue is a bounded research question, not a finding:
did a named, date-bounded stress-knowledge object reach an authority accountable
for a then-current validation burden, change or challenge that burden before
the relevant decision, and remain visibly unresolved or incorrectly closed?

This is more discriminating than prior attention plus later harm. It requires
independent knowledge, custody, authority, burden, timing, stop, disposition,
and causal evidence in each case.

## Absorber Result

California and I-35W defeat a generic stress-knowledge mechanism. Equipment,
fuel, gas-electric, planning, operating, resource-adequacy, nuclear-hazard,
design-basis, structural-capacity, design, rating, inspection, construction-
load, transportation, engineering, regulatory, and legal explanations remain
separate and available to absorb the proposed link.

## Disposition

`narrow`.

Continue only the exact decision-time translation question above. Stop using
comparison count, prior-attention language, institutional plurality, severe
outcome, or later correction as support. The bounded next search is for one
complete source-preserving trace in Texas or Fukushima; failure to reconstruct
that trace is evidence against continuing the lineage, not a reason to add a
new case.

## Evidence Still Required

For one case, name the stress-knowledge object and date; its source and
custodian; the accountable recipient and authority then in force; the current
validation burden it could change; the decision and timing; the stop,
disposition, or closure state; and source-backed causal relevance. Record every
unsupported link as unknown and preserve the narrower domain account.

## Falsifier

Retire the narrowed hypothesis if a complete trace cannot be supported without
borrowing facts across cases; if prior attention never reached the relevant
authority or validation burden; if the burden was timely and adequately
closed; or if the event remains better explained by the preserved domain
absorber without an additional decision-time translation failure.

## Candidate Effect

Narrow one public-source stress-knowledge hypothesis without changing schema,
remedy, acceptance, receiver work, or external action.

## Frontier Verification

Frontier review retained the candidate's comparison inventory, absorber list,
and decision-time authority emphasis. It repaired unclosed frontmatter,
replaced “unproved status” with a testable residue, changed the disposition
from broad continuation to narrowing, and corrected the falsifier so adequate
timely closure counts against the failure hypothesis.
