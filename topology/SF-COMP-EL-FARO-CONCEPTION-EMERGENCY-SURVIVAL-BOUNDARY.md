# El Faro / Conception Emergency-Readiness And Survival Boundary

## Boundary

shared emergency-readiness or survival relation. It keeps El Faro's hurricane,
weather, flooding, vessel-state, damage-control, muster, and survival-craft

## Decision, Counterevidence, And Falsifier

Decision: `preserve_bounded_emergency_survival_comparison`.

The comparison preserves a narrow residue: delayed recognition, emergency
readiness, physical protection, operator oversight, and affected standing can
be compared only while their source-local objects remain distinct. Storm and
fire, damage control and escape, survival craft and egress, and different
regulatory regimes are counterevidence to one shared mechanism.

Falsify the positive residue if closer official-source review removes the
source-backed delayed-recognition or operator-oversight object from either
record. Reject any stronger comparison unless both sources establish the same
bounded custody, accountable authority, disposition, implementation, and
control-effect relation.
