records support one shared hazardous-material custody-to-authority mechanism.

## Decision, Counterevidence, And Falsifier

Decision: `preserve_separate_hazardous_material_authority_phases`.

Both cases involve hazardous-material consequences, but their event,
containment, information, and authority phases are not interchangeable.
Different precipitating mechanics, different decision windows, and East
Palestine's explicit later incident-command authority are counterevidence to a
shared custody-to-authority mechanism based only on hazardous-material release
and severe community consequence.

Reopen a positive comparison only if source-local evidence in both cases
identifies the same bounded relation among material information, custody,
accountable authority, disposition, stop condition, and causal or control
effect without borrowing a link across records. Falsify this negative result
if closer official-source review supplies those complete links in both cases;
narrow either record if it changes a source fact or unknown preserved here.
