## Disposition

`shared_information_to_authority_pressure`.

Both records preserve technically specialized information crossing role
boundaries before a consequential authority decision. That is a provisional
comparison pressure, not recurrence, one custody path, one authority topology,
or one causal mechanism.

## Shared Residue Or Refusal

The residue is limited to whether technically specialized information became
one sufficiently bounded decision object for the authority acting at the
relevant time. The comparison refuses relations based on event sequence,
severity, contractor involvement, BP identity, corporate structure, or later
investigation.

## Material Differences

East Palestine concerns post-derailment incident command with one named on-
scene authority. Macondo concerns pre-blowout test acceptance across distributed
operational and oversight paths. Their evidence objects, decision phases,
standing, absorbers, and known authority facts remain distinct.

## Counterevidence And Alternatives

authority transitions remain unknown. Rail detection, hazardous-materials
response, well design, cementing, drilling, and other domain accounts may
absorb the stronger relation. Later investigation does not prove decision-time
knowledge.
