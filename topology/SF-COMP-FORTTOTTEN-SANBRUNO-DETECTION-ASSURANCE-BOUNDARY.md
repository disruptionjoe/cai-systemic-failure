# Fort Totten / San Bruno Detection-Assurance Boundary

## Boundary

This comparison preserves a bounded relation between Fort Totten's capable but
incompletely institutionalized enhanced field test in `NTSB/RAR-10/02` and San
Bruno's selected integrity-assessment method that could not detect the seam-
weld defect in `NTSB/PAR-11/01`.

It does not infer recurrence, one detection-assurance mechanism, complete
custody, shared authority, relative causal weight, schema pressure, blame,
duty, remedy, acceptance, receiver work, liability, health causation, or
external action.

## Decision, Counterevidence, And Falsifier

Decision: `prepare_bounded_detection_assurance_comparison` while preserving
separate test and assessment phases.

The reports support one bounded comparison: an assurance method's technical
capability and its actual institutional use are separate from one another and
from the physical condition under review. Fort Totten exposes a capable test
that was not institutionalized; San Bruno exposes an assessment method unable
to detect the relevant defect. Their different infrastructure, information,
method, implementation, recognition, oversight, and standing objects are
counterevidence to recurrence or one shared mechanism.

Falsify or materially narrow the comparison if closer official-source review
shows that the Fort Totten enhanced test would not have identified the faulty
circuit or that the San Bruno method could detect the seam-weld defect. Reject
any stronger comparison that imports a common custody path, authority,
correction, implementation, causal weight, or control effect.
