## Disposition

`bounded_visibility_pattern_candidate`.

The supported common residue is narrow: decision-relevant contradiction
evidence was not preserved as one fully visible accountable review object in
either bounded synthesis. The cases do not yet establish a recurring custody,
authority, disposition, or stop mechanism.

## Shared Residue

Boeing preserves fragmented changed-function, assumption, delegated-review,
and integration evidence. Challenger preserves a technical objection whose
recommendation was reversed and not completely communicated to higher launch-
decision levels. Their bounded relation is contradiction visibility before a
consequential decision, not shared domain, severity, delegated actors, or later
investigation.

## Material Differences

Boeing is a bounded positive certification record; Challenger is a false-
negative field-shape pressure test. Their vehicles, hazards, evidence objects,
decision moments, source limits, affected standing, and authority topologies
remain distinct.

## Scope Refused

complete accident chain, borrowed custody or authority, sixth positive record,
schema pressure, remedy, receiver work, and external action.

each case for the contradiction object, custody, accountable recipient and
authority, decision-time validation burden, disposition, timely unresolved
stop or closure, and causal relevance. One case cannot fill another's gaps.
