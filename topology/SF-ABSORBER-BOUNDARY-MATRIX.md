not replace their source sets or detailed gate results. It preserves their
different narrower explanations while making the shared refusal posture
legible. It creates no new evidence, field, remedy, domain conclusion,
acceptance threshold, or external action.

## Shared refusal shape

Each case may contain familiar cues such as multiple owners, review gaps,
changed conditions, warnings, correction routes, or severe consequences. Those
cues are not sufficient by themselves. The candidate refuses positive
qualification when the public official source set supports the narrower
explanation and no qualifying old-assumption-reuse residue remains.

A refused case may remain an absorber, lead, inquiry, separate topology
question, or source-gated pressure. Refusal does not erase affected standing,
settle every uncertainty, prescribe a remedy, or turn negative evidence into
support for the candidate.

## Distinctions that must remain visible

Warning/communication, single-owner execution, infrastructure capacity,
resource adequacy, and new-design/change-control remain five different
explanations. Their public-source limits, affected systems, uncertainty,
correction routes, and domain boundaries remain in the underlying records.
