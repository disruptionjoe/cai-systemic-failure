## Verdict

`current_bounded_corpus_sufficient_for_partial_trace`.

The corpus supports a bounded recommendation-to-reversal-to-incomplete-
visibility residue. It does not support a complete custody, recipient,
authority, disposition, stop, or causal chain. Institutional plurality cannot
fill those missing links.

## Supported Residue

The retained source-backed sequence is technical concern, a no-launch
recommendation below prior temperature experience, management reversal, and
incomplete communication to higher launch-decision levels. This is partial
trace evidence, not a complete decision reconstruction.

## Missing Links

Exact dissent date, object custody, each recipient transition, then-current
authority, complete disposition chain, accountable unresolved stop, and a
complete causal bridge remain unknown.

## Domain Absorbers

Launch safety, spaceflight, engineering, management, regulatory, legal, and
public-safety explanations remain sovereign. The partial trace does not replace
them with a generic review-chain conclusion.

## Next Source Gate

A later source-local review may test one selected dissent object against exact
page or section evidence for custody, recipient, authority, disposition, stop,
and causal relevance. Until then, every missing link remains `unknown`.

## Falsifier

Downgrade this result to `current_bounded_corpus_insufficient` if the cited
owner record does not support the recommendation, reversal, or incomplete-
visibility sequence. Upgrade beyond partial only if source-local evidence
independently supports the missing custody, authority, disposition, stop, and
causal links without borrowing facts across institutions.
