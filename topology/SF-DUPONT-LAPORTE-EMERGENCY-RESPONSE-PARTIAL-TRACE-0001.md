# DuPont La Porte Emergency-Response Partial Trace

## Boundary

This artifact preserves only the emergency-response sequence supported by the
bounded DuPont La Porte record and direct review of CSB Investigation Report
`2015-01-I-TX`. It keeps the distress call, technical support, incident
command, responder entry, navigation, air monitoring, hot-zone control,
