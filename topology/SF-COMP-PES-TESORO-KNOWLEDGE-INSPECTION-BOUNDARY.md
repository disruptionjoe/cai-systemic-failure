# PES / Tesoro Knowledge-To-Inspection Boundary

## Boundary

This comparison tests only a bounded relation between technical knowledge and
installed-equipment-specific measurement or inspection in the Philadelphia
Energy Solutions HF-corrosion and Tesoro Anacortes HTHA records. The damage
mechanisms, material limits, predictive guidance, measurement problems,
inspection techniques, operating phases, standing, and later control objects
remain distinct.

It does not equate the incidents; infer recurrence, one root cause, a shared
knowledge, authority, management, inspection, or remedy mechanism; accept a
schema; assign blame or duty; create receiver work; make an HF, exposure,
health, or environmental conclusion; or authorize external action.

## Decision, Counterevidence, And Falsifier

Decision: `bounded_knowledge_to_installed_equipment_relation`.

The reports support a bounded relation: materially relevant technical
knowledge did not produce complete installed-equipment-specific
characterization and inspection before either incident. Different damage
mechanisms, guidance objects, measurement gaps, inspection methods, work
phases, consequences, and unresolved decision chains are counterevidence to a
recurrence claim or one shared causal, authority, management, or remedy
mechanism.

Withdraw the relation if closer official-source review shows that either case
lacked the described pre-incident technical knowledge or that the relevant
installed equipment had been completely characterized and inspected under
that knowledge before the event. Narrow either side if source review changes a
named material, condition, measurement, inspection, standing, or later-
control fact. Strengthen no mechanism claim without complete source-local
knowledge custody, accountable review, authority, disposition,
implementation, relative-causal-weight, and control-effect chains for both
incidents.

## Result

The comparison closes the prepared gate with one bounded relation and explicit
case separation. It creates no recurrence, shared inspection, management, or
authority mechanism, schema pressure, blame, duty, remedy, acceptance,
receiver work, HF or health conclusion, environmental conclusion, liability,
or external action.
